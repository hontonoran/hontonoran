I have a degree in Japanese translation. In 2019 I took a helpdesk job at Wirex and spent my days answering tickets. By 2021 I was leading that Heldesk team.And now, as a Technical Analyst, I build the systems that route, provision and close a good share of those tickets for us.

My job is process automation from start to finish. I sit with the people who live with a broken process, design the fix, build it and keep it running after launch. Most of it is integration work: I connect Jira, Freshdesk, OneLogin, Microsoft 365 and our own APIs so data moves between them without anyone copying it by hand.

## What I've built

- **Partner companies client onboarding.** 55 client portals set up end to end: SSO, roles, access, support desk. The count goes up every month.
- **Identity and access automation.** One request in, and scheduled runbooks create the accounts, send the invites and update the ticket across SSO, the helpdesk and mailing lists.
- **AI assisted review.** A service that reads customer documents with an AI model and lays out the evidence for a human reviewer, who makes the decision.
- **Request-to-decision workflows.** Intake forms, approval flows and status sync between Jira, SharePoint and the helpdesk, so nobody chases a request by email.
- **2,000+ Jira automation rules** from my years running the helpdesk.

I work in Azure Functions and Automation runbooks, Power Automate, Jira Automation and Python. Next for me: more AI integration, putting language models into workflows where plain code can't read the input.
